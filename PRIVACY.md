# NotiFilter Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 17 October 2026*

NotiFilter does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **What the app reads:** once you grant Notification access, the app reads the title and text of notifications so it can compare them with your filter rules.
- **Where that data goes:** nowhere. The app has no internet permission, so Android prevents it from making network connections. Your rules and the lists of recent and filtered notifications are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.
- **Export:** if you choose *Export rules*, the app writes your rules (not your notifications) to a file at a location you pick. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
- **Deleting your data:** use *Clear* on the Recent and Filtered tabs, delete rules in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
- **Third parties:** none. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking libraries.

The full source code is public, so you can check every statement above.
